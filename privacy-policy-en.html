CuddleWall
Privacy policy

エビ天 · 2026-10-11

Scope and developer

This policy covers the Google Play version of CuddleWall by エビ天. It proposes focus rules from usage records. There are no accounts, ads, external analytics SDKs, location or Wi-Fi conditions, or map requests.

On-device information

App names, package names and types, OS usage time and opening or screen-event timestamps support target selection, statistics, suggestions and limits. Accessibility processes the current app, browser address bars and controls to identify targets you select. Other apps’ content and input, screen images, full browsing URLs and search terms are not stored or transmitted. Selected domains and budget counters are stored locally.

Optional analysis and helpers

Detailed web and short-video analysis is optional. Available domains, types and times are recorded; web analysis uses only verified normal tabs. Private and unknown tabs are excluded from detailed analysis. Counters for your selected limits are handled separately. Notification and audio helpers use sender and media-control information without storing or transmitting notification text or playback content.

Communication and exports

The Play version has no INTERNET permission and does not automatically send usage, rules, records or passwords to the developer or third parties. You choose where to export settings; a cloud destination has its own privacy practices. Policy links open in an external browser. Contact is sent through your email app with no automatic sending or attachments.

Retention and deletion

Analysis records are retained for up to 90 days; detailed intervals are pruned after about 3 days. Rules, settings, purpose classifications, consent and password verifiers remain until deleted. Counters for active limits remain until those limits end. The password itself is not stored or exported. Stop or erase analysis in Settings → Record management. Clear data or uninstall in Android Settings to remove all app records. OS usage history and your exported files are managed separately.

Choices and permissions

Usage and accessibility each require a separate purpose explanation, affirmative consent and OS permission. Optional helper permissions are needed only for their selected features. OS permission withdrawal, data clearing and uninstall remain available. You adopt and start rules; adaptive plans adjust future budgets only within the range you approve.

Contact and changes

Contact エビ天 at ebifri.dev@gmail.com. Contact details and messages you send are used to respond. Policy changes are reflected in this page’s update date and the in-app explanation.
